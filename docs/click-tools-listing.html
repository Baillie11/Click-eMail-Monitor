Click Mail Monitor is a free browser-based email workspace from Click eCommerce. Bring compatible hosted email accounts together, read and search imported messages, keep track of new mail and prepare replies for review—all while keeping your existing email address and provider.

What can you do with Click Mail Monitor?

  • Connect your existing mailboxes: Add compatible IMAP/SMTP accounts, including hosted email from providers such as VentraIP and OrangeHost.
  • Read and search in one place: Browse imported messages across connected accounts or filter by mailbox.
  • Check for new mail: Let scheduled monitoring run in the background, use Check Now for an individual mailbox or Check All Now from the inbox.
  • See mailbox health at a glance: View connection status, the last successful check, the next scheduled check and the number of imported emails.
  • Monitor what matters: Set rules using details such as sender, recipient, subject or unread status and review matching alerts.
  • Prepare replies with control: Draft responses and review them before approving a send. Sending requires your approval.
  • Organise team access: Use workspaces and mailbox permissions to control which connected mailboxes team members can access.

Connect compatible email accounts to ChatGPT

Click Mail Monitor includes an authenticated ChatGPT connection for reading and searching authorised email and preparing replies. A separate connection setup is required, and availability depends on your ChatGPT account and its support for custom apps. It is not currently a publicly listed ChatGPT directory plugin. Your mailbox password is entered in Click Mail Monitor’s secure setup form, not in a chat.

Get started

  1. Open the free web app and create an account.
  2. Verify your email address, sign in and connect a compatible mailbox using its IMAP/SMTP settings.
  3. Test the connection, then choose Check Now to import messages.
  4. Read your inbox, add monitoring rules and connect additional accounts as needed.

You do not need to install a desktop program to use the hosted app. Native Gmail and Outlook/Hotmail sign-in integrations are not currently available.

Your email stays with your provider

The app connects to your existing mail provider and stores an imported working copy within its configured import and retention limits. Imported email counts may therefore differ from the total messages held by your provider. Mailbox credentials are encrypted at rest, connections use secure mail protocols, and account access can be revoked.
