# User guide

## Connect and find mail

Register, verify your email and sign in. Open Mailboxes and enter the secure IMAP/SMTP settings supplied by your provider. Test connection checks authentication only; it sends no email and does not import messages. Progress and results appear beneath the button.

Wait for the scheduled check, then use Inbox to search and read imported messages. The last/next check is shown in Mailboxes. Initial imports cover a bounded date window, so older messages may not appear. Pausing a mailbox stops its scheduled processing.

## Monitoring and replies

Create monitoring rules for relevant sender, recipient, subject or unread criteria. Matching messages generate alerts. Review drafted replies in Review queue, including recipients and attachments. Approve only after checking the final content. Editing invalidates approval. Failed or uncertain delivery requires review before any further attempt.

## ChatGPT

Connect the deployed MCP app as described in CHATGPT.md. Ask it to list mailboxes, search imported messages or read a selected email. Example: “Find emails about my booking.” The worker continues independently of ChatGPT. Access renews automatically for up to 90 days after connecting. Reconnect after that period, revocation or password reset.
